# AltStore: after AltServer 1.8.2, users report a new Apple sign-in error (-80046) instead of -80045

- Source issue: https://github.com/altstoreio/AltStore/issues/1840 (open at check time; no labels; opened 2026-10-21 20:23 UTC; 5 comments)
- Related reports: https://github.com/altstoreio/AltStore/issues/1839 ("The provided anisette data is invalid", open) and the long-running https://github.com/altstoreio/AltStore/issues/1828 (error -80045, open, 89 comments, 109 reactions)
- Open pull requests that propose a fix for -80045: https://github.com/altstoreio/AltStore/pull/1835 (base `classic`) and https://github.com/rileytestut/AltSign/pull/61 (neither merged at check time)
- Our earlier note on -80045: findings/2026-10-15T1230Z-altstore-error-80045.md in this repo
- Project: https://github.com/altstoreio/AltStore (AGPL-3.0, 14,708 stars per the API; latest release v2.3, 2026-09-30)
- Checked: 2026-10-22 04:35 UTC

## Problem
AltStore lets people install and refresh apps on iPhones with a free Apple ID using a companion program called AltServer. Since about 12 October, issue #1828 has collected reports of error -80045 on refresh and install. The new issue reporter says they installed the newly released AltServer 1.8.2, which "is supposed to fix error -80045". They still cannot install AltStore, and now get -80046 ("AltServer could not sign in with your Apple ID. This action cannot be completed at this time."). Four commenters say they see the same: one after entering a 2FA code, one on the Windows version that also cannot update, one on a Mac, and one who says it "just started today". Commenters on #1828 say they got -80046 after fresh reinstalls. A commenter on #1839 says "503 just means you need to wait", which is their opinion.

## What the project and contributors said
- On #1828 (2026-10-13) a staff member who identifies as the community manager and developer-relations person wrote that it "seems to be an Apple-side issue", and that they were using the reports to monitor and decide whether a fix is needed. That is the only comment from someone with a repository role on #1828 that we saw. We could not tell from the thread what AltServer 1.8.2 contains.
- Pull request #1835, by an account the API marks as a collaborator, says Apple now rejects anisette data (device identity data sent at sign-in) generated by Android's CoreADI, that it accepts Windows anisette data sent with that client's own client info and user agent, and that the change was tested on an iPhone Air with a free Apple ID. It depends on AltSign PR #61. Both were open and unmerged at check time. We have not reviewed the code.
- Commenters on #1828 mention unofficial builds and a way to sign with Xcode. We do not relay those. They are third-party builds and workarounds that we have not vetted, and anything that asks for an Apple ID deserves caution.

## What this does not tell you
- It does not say what -80046 means. We found no explanation from the project. The error text is generic.
- It does not say AltServer 1.8.2 caused it. The reporters say they saw -80046 after installing it, and several say the failure began on the same day, so it may also be a change on Apple's side. Nobody in the thread confirmed either.
- It does not say whether an official fix is on the way. The PRs for -80045 are open, and no maintainer reply on #1840 was visible at check time.
- It is not advice to try unofficial builds or sign in elsewhere with your Apple ID.

## Honest limits
- We did not run AltServer or AltStore, did not check which release is 1.8.2 beyond what commenters say (the repository's latest release listed by the API is v2.3), and did not read the screenshots.
- Statements about Apple's behavior come from the PR description and commenters.
- The picture may have changed since 04:35 UTC.
